About us

Fitness feels like family.

Founded in 2014, F2L is a community-based gym in Kogarah. We help people improve their fitness in a friendly and supportive environment.

Our mission

Stronger, healthier and more confident.

Our approach combines friendly coaching, practical movement and community support. Members are encouraged to train consistently and progress at a comfortable pace.
